# AquaAd brand guidelines

Source of truth for the AquaAd website and admin. Tokens live at the top of `dist/index.html` (primitive, semantic, component).

## Positioning
AquaAd turns everyday 500 ml water bottles into a brand surface. Brands pick the panels, partners make and distribute the bottles, people see the message in daily life.

## Voice
- Clear and practical. Say what happens next, in plain words.
- Confident, not hyped. No invented numbers, customers or reviews.
- Say "bottle", "panel", "artwork". Avoid jargon.
- Always be honest about what is provisional (sizes, dieline).

## Messaging
- Headline: The billboard people carry.
- Statement: You can skip an ad. You can't skip being thirsty.
- Audiences: brands (advertise), venues (distribute), manufacturers and water partners (partner), investors.
- Calls to action: Design your bottle, Distribute with us, Become a partner, Request investor information.

## Visual identity
Deep water, one loud colour, very large type. It should feel like a media brand, not a software template.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Abyss | #04141C | dark sections, text on light and on orange |
| Paper / Ice | #F8FAF9 / #EDF3F2 | light sections |
| Signal | #FF5A1F | primary actions, the cap, the "ad" in the wordmark. Always dark text on it |
| Sea | #7FD4E6 | accent on dark only |

- Display: Bricolage Grotesque 700, tight tracking, very large. One italic word in Instrument Serif for emphasis.
- Body: Geist. Specs and labels: Geist Mono, uppercase.
- Hairlines and big numbers instead of boxes and icons. Pills for controls, 24 px radius for cards.
- The 3D bottle (`dist/bottle3d.js`) is the hero. Artwork colours must stay true, so no tone mapping.
- Targets at least 44 px. Visible focus ring. Respect reduced motion. Moving content can be paused.
- Avoid: stock SaaS icon cards, blue-on-white gradients, emoji icons, invented numbers or customers.

## Bottle spec (provisional)
500 ml PET bottle, 73 mm wide. Label area 73 x 170 mm: two panels of 73 x 85 mm per side, or one whole side. Seven panels are for sale; the lower back is reserved for AquaAd information. Confirm with the bottle supplier before production.
